# Proof-Writing Sources and Provenance

The derivation workflow incorporates MIT-licensed material from Moran Koren's
[`theorist-toolbox`](https://github.com/morankor/theorist-toolbox); see
`../LICENSE.math-proof`. The local version replaces mechanical requirements with
audience-calibrated rigor and separates generated proofs from independent verification.

The readability protocol also draws on general advice associated with John M. Lee,
*Some Remarks on Writing Mathematical Proofs*; discussions of proof readability on MathOverflow
and Mathematics Stack Exchange; Hamilton College's proof-writing guidance; Ted Sundstrom's
*Mathematical Reasoning*; and the broader writing traditions of Halmos, Knuth, Higham, Lamport,
and Serre. Treat these as provenance and reading leads, not citations for mathematical claims.
