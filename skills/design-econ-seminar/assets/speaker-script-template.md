# Speaker Script Template

```markdown
# Speaker Notes: <deck name>

Italic text gives delivery cues rather than spoken prose. Explain equations in words rather than reading the symbols.

**Estimated presentation time:** about **<total> minutes**.

## Opening - Title Slide

*Slide: 1:00 | Cumulative: 1:00*

- Opening sentence and the paper's broad purpose.
- The two or three questions organizing the talk.
- Short roadmap.

## Slide 1 - <Exact slide title>

*Slide: 1:30 | Cumulative: 2:30*

- Main claim.
- Explanation of the evidence, equation, or visual.
- Economic interpretation or qualification.
- Specific handoff to the next part of the argument.

*Optional backups (exact clicker labels): **<label>** (<purpose>).*

## Optional Time Cuts

- Slides that can be summarized without breaking the argument.

## Optional Backup-Slide Explainers

- Concise answer supported by each useful backup slide.
```

Use the exact slide titles and clicker labels from the deck. Do not copy placeholder phrasing into the finished script.
