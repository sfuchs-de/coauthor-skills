# Sources

- Jonathan Dingel, [Advice for PhD students and recommended resources](http://www.jdingel.com/teaching/advice.html): timing, claim-based titles, assertion-evidence design, distinct presentation figures, accessible color, and discussant discipline.
- Jonathan Dingel, [Recurring presentation feedback](http://www.jdingel.com/teaching/spring2025_presentation_feedback_summary.html): lead with the question, reach design and results quickly, show raw evidence first, define notation, and use one clear message per slide.
- Jonathan Schwabish, “An Economist's Guide to Visualizing Data,” *Journal of Economic Perspectives* 28(1), 2014.
- Jesse Shapiro, “How to Give an Applied Micro Talk.”
- Edward Tufte, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*.

The skill converts these principles into an operational audit and does not reproduce the source materials.
