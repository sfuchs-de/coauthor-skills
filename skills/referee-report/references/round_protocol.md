# Initial And Revision Round Protocol

## Initial Round

The initial report should establish the paper's contribution and identify the few issues that
determine whether its main claims are credible.

- Use concise mode by default: target 800-950 words and treat 750-1,000 as the hard range. Use
  standard mode (target 950-1,080; hard range 900-1,200) for an explicitly comprehensive report or
  several independently decisive problems.
- Use two to four summary paragraphs.
- Include two or three major comments by default and no more than four in standard mode.
- Keep smaller comments short and non-blocking.
- Reserve the recommendation for the editor note.

## Revision Round

Read the prior report, author response, and revised manuscript together. Build an internal matrix:

| Prior concern | Author response | Revised location | Status | Remaining action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | resolved / partly resolved / unresolved / not assessable | ... |

The author-facing revision report should:

- Target 450-650 words while treating 400-700 as a hard acceptance range unless several central
  concerns remain.
- State briefly what the revision changed.
- Close resolved issues explicitly without restating the original critique at length.
- Explain the precise residual problem for partly resolved issues.
- Ask for the smallest remaining action or qualification.
- Avoid new extensions that are unrelated to a still-open claim.

A new concern is admissible only if the revision creates it, newly supplied evidence reveals it, or
it is necessary to evaluate an unresolved prior claim. Record why it is new.

## Editor Note

The editor note should be 180-300 words and include:

1. the contribution and current state of the manuscript;
2. the one or two considerations driving the recommendation;
3. whether those issues are fatal, addressable, or largely resolved; and
4. the recommendation.

Check that the recommendation matches the severity language in the author report. Do not copy the
full technical discussion into the note.

Calibrate the recommendation to the minimum required rebuild. If the treatment, baseline estimator,
comparison group, and principal interpretation all need replacement, explain privately why a normal
revision remains credible or recommend rejection. Do not let promising data alone convert a redesign
into a revise-and-resubmit recommendation.

Count the final deliverable after drafting. A report outside the selected mode's hard range must be
revised before delivery; a model estimate or visual page estimate is not a substitute for a word
count.
