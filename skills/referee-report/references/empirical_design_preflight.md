# Empirical Design Preflight

Use this private pass for empirical papers before selecting major comments. Its purpose is to
determine what the headline coefficient literally estimates and to catch internal inconsistencies
before broader discussion of contribution, robustness, or mechanisms.

## Estimand Reconstruction

Record, in compact working notes:

- the unit of observation and risk set;
- the level at which treatment and outcomes vary;
- the treated and comparison observations;
- the variation remaining after each fixed effect;
- whether any stated fixed effect should absorb the treatment;
- whether one unit can enter both treated and comparison groups;
- whether the comparison group is affected through substitution, spillovers, anticipation, or
  equilibrium adjustment;
- how zeros, entry, exit, and sample selection enter the estimating sample; and
- the literal economic interpretation of the coefficient.

Do not stop at `unclear` when the paper supplies enough notation to resolve the issue. Work through
the indexing. If the treatment and fixed effects are inconsistent as written, make that the leading
candidate concern. If crucial information is absent, name the missing object and the smallest
disclosure that would resolve it.

## Headline Arithmetic

For the two or three results carrying the paper:

1. Reconstruct at least one reported economic magnitude from the coefficient, transformation,
   standard deviation or treatment movement, and outcome units.
2. Check logs, percentage changes, elasticities, standardized coefficients, and counterfactual
   baselines explicitly.
3. Compare the baseline magnitude with the main fixed-effect or sample alternatives.
4. Reconcile the abstract, main text, tables, figures, notes, and appendix.
5. Check whether quantities or values aggregate objects with compatible units.

Treat a failed reconstruction, an implausible magnitude, or a contradiction across exhibits as a
substantive finding when it affects the headline claim. Do not turn harmless rounding differences
into comments.

## Claim-To-Test Mapping

Translate each central interpretation into its closest observable implication. Prefer a direct
within-unit or accounting test over a broad robustness menu. Examples include:

- diversion requires gains in one market to coincide with losses in the alleged source markets;
- a mechanism should concentrate where its exposure or mediator is present;
- entry and exit claims require outcomes that retain zeros;
- a policy classification should map to the policy rule rather than a convenient proxy; and
- a destination effect requires destination-level identifying variation.

If the evidence establishes only differential growth, correlation, or consistency with a
mechanism, narrow the requested interpretation accordingly.

## Recommendation Calibration

Before writing the private editor synthesis, ask whether the concern can be resolved while
preserving the baseline treatment, estimating equation, and principal interpretation.

- A targeted diagnostic, corrected inference procedure, or defensible qualification is normally
  materially addressable.
- Replacement of one component may still support revision when the remaining design and result are
  credible.
- Simultaneous replacement of the treatment, baseline estimator, comparison group, and headline
  interpretation is normally a redesign. Calibrate the recommendation accordingly, even when the
  data and underlying project are promising.

This is a judgment rule, not an automatic rejection formula. Journal standards, available data,
and the credibility of a feasible replacement still matter.
