# Quantitative Model Audit

Use this private pass for calibrated or estimated structural papers whose contribution rests on a
counterfactual. It is an economic interpretation audit, not a request to reproduce every equation
or expose a checklist in the report.

## Result-Driving Primitives

Identify the few elasticities, wedges, costs, or residuals that determine the headline result. For
each one, ask what economic behavior it represents, which omitted mechanisms it may absorb, and
which moments identify or calibrate it. Then ask whether the counterfactual itself could change that
object. A parameter estimated under one market structure, capacity regime, or adjustment horizon is
not automatically policy invariant.

## Data And Market Correspondence

Pin down the observed object before accepting the calibration: quote or transaction, payment or
revenue, posted or negotiated price, shipment or route, physical quantity or expenditure. Check
that the service and market match the model, including mode, product, equipment, customer segment,
spot versus contract, geography, and time horizon. A mismatch may require a narrower interpretation
or sensitivity exercise; it is a major problem only when it drives the central quantitative claim.

## Shock, Scope, And Adjustment

Trace the empirical source of the policy shock. A border, reform, or infrastructure indicator may
also absorb regulation, geography, selection, or other costs. Ask whether the model's capacity,
entry, outside options, market power, and network connections are appropriate for the horizon of
the exercise. Tie an omitted margin to the specific counterfactual it can change rather than listing
features found in richer papers.

## Benchmark And Validation

Comparisons across models are informative only when they use comparable baseline targets or are
independently recalibrated; otherwise differences can reflect different starting economies. Prefer
validation of the claimed comparative static or propagation mechanism to a fit in levels. Useful
tests expose the model to a credible disturbance and compare changes in targeted and untargeted
markets. Inspect figures for flattening, kinks, asymmetry, or influential observations that may
reveal a missing mechanism even when aggregate fit looks good.

## Translation To The Report

Keep only findings that change identification, interpretation, or a headline counterfactual. Fold
related symptoms into one economic objection with one primary request and one stopping rule.
Normally no more than one major comment should be devoted to quantitative scope or closure unless
the paper is primarily methodological. Write the result in ordinary first-person referee prose;
do not show this audit as a table, scorecard, or checklist.
