# Repair the Reasoning, Not Just the Vocabulary

Use these contrasts only for a defect actually present in the passage. The
examples are original, fictional illustrations of an editing decision; none is
quoted from the corpus. Preserve all required results and qualifications in the
surrounding text. The point is not to copy the improved sentences or impose their
length, but to make the economic inference explicit.

## A Literature Paragraph Still Reads Like a List

Weak move: describe what each named paper does in successive sentences, then say
the current paper also connects theory and data. The last statement does not
explain the connection or the difference.

Better move: organize the references around the measured object and the
restriction that gives it meaning. For example:

> An expenditure share can summarize trade gains within a specified class of
> models. The restriction does the work: observed purchases alone do not give a
> welfare interpretation. Our calculation separates valuation from recovery.
> Initial purchases value supplied price changes, while the demand restriction
> permits income and prices to be inferred from spending.

The full paragraph still needs each supplied reference's actual role. Do not
assign a shared result to different papers merely to smooth the sentence. In the
source analogues, K01 p3 distinguishes primitive and aggregate restrictions;
D01 pp13/15 states what the theoretical results permit the empirical analysis
to recover; A01 pp3/20 connects spatial objects to specific recovery operations.
Those are distinct contributions, not interchangeable citations.

## A Conclusion Restates That Assumptions Matter

Weak move: finish with several sentences that the model is conditional, not
universally valid, and needs further work. Such sentences may be true but leave
the original economic question unanswered.

Better move: state the supported answer and the precise unresolved difference.
For example:

> Spatial rankings are similar across the income specifications even though the
> mean changes sign. The evidence therefore leaves the aggregate gain dependent
> on income recovery, rather than establishing that visitors benefit residents
> on average.

This retains an adverse sensitivity rather than hiding it in a footnote. It does
not call stable rankings causal, accurate, or independently validated. Attach
other indispensable scope limits to the outcomes they qualify. Do not delete
substantive facts just because the ending would be shorter without them.

D01 pp33/34 returns from measured income to the mechanism the model can explain.
K04 p41 moves from a model-class limitation to the economic distinction that
remains informative. A01 p30 concludes a tools paper by describing the operations
its framework permits. The right ending depends on what this paper establishes.

## Smooth Prose Accidentally Strengthens a Claim

Weak move: replace "computed" with "optimal," or turn a possible explanation for
a pattern into its demonstrated cause. Both can sound cleaner while changing the
scientific claim.

Better move: name the object actually supported. For example:

> The lower gain comes from the changed income allocation; the cost component is
> unchanged. This accounts for the numerical difference, but does not establish
> which allocation describes residents' income correctly.

That is an accounting interpretation, not a causal test of the allocation. A
proposed mechanism should remain a hypothesis if the supplied evidence does not
identify it. Keep exact units, optimization status, and the distinction between
local valuation and finite predictions throughout the revision.

After making a repair, read the whole paragraph. If the new sentence leaves no
unresolved economic question for the next sentence to take up, remove a redundant
summary or connect the next claim through its actual object. Do not manufacture
an estimate or a mechanism to fill the gap.
