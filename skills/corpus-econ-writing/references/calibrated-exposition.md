# Match the Economic Task

Use the explanatory practices in the named samples, not their signature wording.
There is no single prose template shared by an empirical introduction, a formal
result, and a quantitative comparison. Read one relevant paper card or worked
pattern before drafting a substantial passage. Source versions and coauthorship
limit individual attribution; resemblance concerns the writing operation.

## Move an Economic Object Forward

Each paragraph should change what the reader knows about the question. A known
object can become a choice, a relationship, something measured, or a result to
interpret. Let the next paragraph take up the unresolved object. Do not make the
publication text narrate a claim ledger, checklist, drafting pass, or verification
procedure. Those are internal editing tools.

Use ordinary signposts when they orient the reader. The reference papers do use
phrases such as announcing a comparison or a next step. The defect is an empty
announcement, not the presence of a signpost. Preserve a natural mix of sentence
lengths; do not split every explanation into short statements or force every
paragraph into the same question-mechanism-limit pattern.

## Introductions and Abstracts

Make the economic question and obstacle specific, then explain why the available
data and method answer it. Connect a contribution to the operation it permits,
rather than listing research activities. When results are supplied, state the
economic answer and the decisive limitation together. Do not replace an answer
with a general statement that the method is useful.

Evidence: D01 pp1-3; D02 pp2-4; D03 pp2-3. Read the cards in `library/reviews/`.

## Theory and Spatial Transmission

Establish the choice and timing before the formula. Give a condition the exact
job it performs, then explain the result through the choice or market interaction.
Interpret an initial quantity or share in economic terms. Avoid an equation list
followed by a second prose list of everything the equations do not imply.

For a network, explain who receives a payment and where they spend it before
introducing the operator. Describe the repeated economic action, not just a
sequence of labels. Separate an algebraic solution, a convergent feedback sum,
and an equilibrium derivative when they have different conditions.

Evidence: A01 pp8-9/13; A02 pp16/19; K01 pp7/11/14; K03 p23.

## Data and Identification

Explain which economic object each source observes and why another source is
needed. A coverage difference matters because it changes who or what is measured,
not merely because two files differ. Use institutions and sample construction when
they bear on variation, representativeness, or the interpretation of an estimate.

Connect the threat, controls, remaining variation, and estimated response. When
recovery requires another restriction, explain its role at that point. A fixed
effect that absorbs a common level should have that limit attached to the level
claim, not appear in a detached final warning paragraph.

Evidence: D01 pp2/15/20/25; D02 pp20/32; D04 pp3/22/27.

## Results and Quantitative Comparisons

Lead with the economically important contrast. Use the supplied numbers to
explain its source, not to recite every table column with identical sentences.
Keep required magnitudes, units, and adverse findings. State which component,
ranking, or mean changes and explain the consequence for the original question.

For models, first establish what changes and what remains common. Explain the
choice margin before its parameters. Distinguish calibration targets, predictions,
and untargeted checks. End with the particular comparison still needed, not a
generic warning that the model may be wrong about everything. Never invent a
check's outcome to make the transition smoother.

Evidence: D01 pp32-33; D04 pp27/47; K03 pp25/30; K04 pp15/20/34.

## Literature and Conclusions

Organize comparisons around the economic question, identifying operation, or
restriction. Integrate citations into that argument rather than allocating a
disconnected sentence to each paper. Explain a difference precisely enough that
it changes the interpretation, not merely that this paper studies another setting.

Make the shared economic object or the actual difference do the connecting. A
sequence of author names followed by "we also connect theory and data" is still
a list, even if it is one paragraph. State what an expenditure share, equilibrium
system, or identifying restriction permits the researcher to infer, then locate
the cited work at that inference. Do not invent a common theorem for papers that
perform different tasks. End with the particular unresolved measurement or
restriction relevant to the current paper, not another generic summary of the
connection between theory and evidence.

Conclude with the answer and its mechanism at the scope actually established.
Retain the important unresolved limit. Do not finish with a catalogue of missing
results or unsupported policy advice. A conclusion need not repeat every
qualification already attached to its result.

Choose the ending according to what the paper establishes. A tools paper may
conclude with the operations its framework makes possible; an empirical paper
should return to its economic answer. When the evidence supports a conditional
or adverse answer, make that answer concrete rather than replacing it with the
statement that "restrictions matter." Similar spatial rankings, for example, do
not establish that either set of estimates is accurate. Keep stability, causal
validity, and scope distinct. Do not add a numerical implication or recommendation
merely to give the conclusion a stronger final sentence.

Evidence: K01 p3; A01 pp3/5/30; D01 pp33-34; K04 p41.

For the recurring defects above, see the original worked contrasts in

to paste into unrelated papers.

## Qualifications and Vocabulary

An income-recovery sign reversal belongs next to the mean result. Necessary
timing and exclusion assumptions stay with the claim they support. These are not
expendable caveats. Other qualifications should qualify their own sentence or
paragraph once, rather than forming consecutive generic negative statements.
Where possible, say affirmatively what is measured under the restriction.

Distinguish interpreting an established component from proposing a channel. A
numerical accounting decomposition can explain which component changes; a
plausible story about migration, sorting, or market power is not thereby an
identified mechanism. When the brief supplies only an association, keep that
status while explaining why the possible channel motivates another test.

Use the strict checker before delivering prose. If an absent word has an accurate
attested alternative, rewrite its sentence naturally; do not perform local synonym
replacement that leaves awkward syntax. The corpus has `visitors`, `card`, `bundle`,
and `invertible`, for example, but this does not license related unattested forms.
Necessary terms still require the explicit exception procedure. A smooth sentence
cannot justify changing the population, input, price, or mathematical condition.
