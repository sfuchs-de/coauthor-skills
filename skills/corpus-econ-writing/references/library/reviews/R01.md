## Exposition Assessment
The historical episode supplies identifying variation rather than scenery. Exogenous-characteristic recovery is distinguished from estimation of endogenous externalities. Reduced-form reorientation, structural parameters, and counterfactual predictions occupy separate evidentiary stages.
