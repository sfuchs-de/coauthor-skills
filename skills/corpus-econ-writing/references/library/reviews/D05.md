## Exposition Assessment
Examples explain how theory connects observed outcomes to the desired counterfactual. The valuation argument remains separate from the identification of its inputs. The review essay's metaphors are useful pedagogically, not a template for every research introduction.

## Page-Linked Observations
- `D05-01` / PDF p1 / question: Familiar policy questions motivate a methodological question.
- `D05-02` / PDF p2 / scope: A model is evaluated against its intended answer.
- `D05-03` / PDF p5 / identification: Observed relationships need not identify the desired outcome.
- `D05-04` / PDF p9 / levels: Cross-regional variation cannot establish a nationwide level effect.
- `D05-05` / PDF p13 / envelope: Optimization gives a direct first-order valuation argument.
