# Worked Exposition Patterns

These are original analyses of selected passages, not transcriptions, universal
author habits, or templates to paste unchanged. Page numbers refer to the frozen
source PDFs. Each recommended application still requires the current paper's
scientific argument.

## 1. A Theory Result Becomes an Empirical Task

**Evidence:** [D01, PDF pp13 and 15](reviews/D01.md), observations D01-04 and D01-05.

The model discussion does not end with a formula and a separate estimation roadmap.
A verbal implication gives the formula a purpose; subsequent empirical work uses
that implication to organize what must be measured. The reader can recognize the
same economic object on both sides of the theory-to-data transition.

**Argument map:** economic implication -> formal relationship -> measurable object
-> empirical operation.

**Apply:** end the theory subsection by naming the unobserved response, then begin
the empirical subsection with the data and variation that recover it. Do not use
an appendix reference as the only bridge.

## 2. Generality Is Added in Stages

**Evidence:** [D03, PDF pp7, 19, 22 and 26](reviews/D03.md), D03-03, D03-06 to D03-08;
[R02, PDF pp29 and 49](reviews/R02.md), R02-07 and R02-11.

The reader first learns the economic objects and then the restrictions needed for
a particular task. Representation, identification, and practical recovery are
distinct accomplishments. A computable algorithm is not used as a substitute for
the missing identification argument.

**Argument map:** economy -> representation -> information available -> required
condition -> implementable recovery.

**Apply:** place the assumption immediately before the operation it permits. Explain
why it is needed and what remains general after imposing it. Preserve that scope
in the proposition and interpretation.

## 3. Optimization Explains the Envelope

**Evidence:** [D05, PDF p13](reviews/D05.md), D05-05.

An optimized value is the starting point, not an unexplained derivative. This gives
the reader a reason the direct price effect is sufficient locally. The rhetorical
economy comes from eliminating a separate first-order choice term, not from
eliminating the optimization problem or the conditions for the envelope.

**Argument map:** choice at an optimum -> direct effect of the shock -> local
valuation -> scope of the approximation.

**Apply:** explain the initially optimized choice before differentiating. Interpret
the initial quantity or share afterward. Distinguish local valuation from finite
adjustment; do not imply that quantities stay fixed.

## 4. Two Models Can Share an Organizing System

**Evidence:** [A03, PDF pp9, 15, 21 and 26](reviews/A03.md), A03-03 to A03-05, A03-07.

Parallel models are not left as two disconnected derivations. Their shared objects
are consolidated before moving to empirical tools. Timing restrictions and common
elasticities have separate jobs, so the consolidation does not make the models'
conditions interchangeable.

**Argument map:** variants -> common objects -> equilibrium relationships ->
empirical tools.

**Apply:** introduce a shared notation only after establishing the common economics.
Keep a short closure or assumption comparison alongside it. A common matrix shape
does not make two operators the same economic object.

## 5. A Simple Example Has a Defined Role

**Evidence:** [K01, PDF pp7, 9, 11 and 14](reviews/K01.md), K01-03 to K01-06;
[A01, PDF p17](reviews/A01.md), A01-07.

A simplified economy teaches the mechanism before the general result introduces
its restrictions. The illustration and the theorem perform different tasks. The
transition succeeds when it identifies which insight survives and which new
conditions support it.

**Argument map:** familiar special case -> mechanism -> general primitives ->
aggregate restrictions -> result for a defined shock.

**Apply:** state what the example omits. Return explicitly to the general economy.
Do not call a later parametric recovery formula general merely because the opening
example uses unrestricted preferences.

## 6. A Fixed Effect Is an Object to Interpret

**Evidence:** [R02, PDF p41](reviews/R02.md), R02-14;
[R04, PDF pp3 and 9](reviews/R04.md), R04-02 and R04-04.

An estimated composite is not given the name of a single primitive and left there.
Its components and normalization matter for what can be recovered. A residual's
economic interpretation is a maintained modeling choice, not a measurement supplied
automatically by estimation.

**Argument map:** estimated composite -> economic decomposition -> scale or
restriction -> separately interpreted object.

**Apply:** define what the effect absorbs, show the correction or restriction, and
state what remains unidentified. Carry those limits into observed-income checks
and aggregate welfare interpretation.

## 7. A Data Pattern Motivates, but Does Not Prove, a Mechanism

**Evidence:** [A05, PDF p3](reviews/A05.md), A05-02;
[R06, PDF pp4 and 21](reviews/R06.md), R06-03 and R06-10.

Empirical patterns provide a reason to model choices or spatial forces. They also
receive interpretation in their own right. The exposition separates the observed
pattern from a claim about its underlying cause or microfoundation.

**Argument map:** measured contrast -> plausible economic forces -> unresolved
mechanism -> modeling or identification task.

**Apply:** discuss magnitudes and denominators before explaining what the pattern
suggests. Mention a relevant alternative explanation. Do not describe a descriptive
map as causal evidence merely because the proposed mechanism is intuitive.

## 8. Disagreement Is Part of the Answer

**Evidence:** [D01, PDF p32](reviews/D01.md), D01-08;
[D06, PDF p56](reviews/D06.md), D06-10;
[D04, PDF pp27 and 47](reviews/D04.md), D04-08 and D04-09.

A fit comparison can agree qualitatively yet fail a formal restriction. Another
check can preserve the broad distributional pattern while changing its magnitude.
These are different findings. Neither a good correlation nor a non-rejection alone
establishes that a model is correct for every policy question.

**Argument map:** comparison -> component that agrees -> component that disagrees
-> implication for the claim's scope.

**Apply:** distinguish sign, ranking, magnitude, and precision. State whether the
comparison is targeted or untargeted. Explain the consequence of disagreement,
instead of appending an unspecific claim of robustness.

## 9. Formal Statements Receive Interpretation

**Evidence:** [A06, PDF p19](reviews/A06.md), A06-07;
[R05, PDF p29](reviews/R05.md), R05-08;
[A01, PDF p9](reviews/A01.md), A01-05.

Similar-looking mathematical properties can answer different economic questions.
The surrounding prose distinguishes them rather than using a general adjective
such as well-behaved. An algebraic inverse, an equilibrium uniqueness result, a
convergent expansion, and a dynamic long-run outcome need not be equivalent.

**Argument map:** condition -> exact mathematical implication -> economic meaning
-> separate claim not established by that condition.

**Apply:** give a condition an explicit task. State separately any spectral,
equilibrium, or dynamic property needed later. A sentence about what is not implied
can prevent an otherwise smooth but incorrect transition.

## 10. The Conclusion Preserves the Research Question

**Evidence:** [D01, PDF pp33-34](reviews/D01.md), D01-09 and D01-10;
[K04, PDF p41](reviews/K04.md), K04-11.

The conclusion returns to the result's economic purpose and scope. It does not
turn the measured outcome into another outcome or introduce an application that
needs new evidence. A boundary can be stated plainly without repeating every
qualification in the paper.

**Argument map:** opening question -> answer supported -> mechanism or interpretation
-> important boundary -> useful unresolved question.

**Apply:** compare the conclusion's population, shock, units, and horizon against
the introduction. Retain meaningful limits, but remove administrative descriptions
of the drafting or appendix-building process.
