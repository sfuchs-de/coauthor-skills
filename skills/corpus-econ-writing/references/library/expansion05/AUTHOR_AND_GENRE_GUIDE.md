# Mechanisms, Identification, And Aggregate Interpretation

Use this supplement alongside the preceding argument guide, not as a replacement
for the author’s established voice. The selected studies include published papers,
working manuscripts, a survey, and a short proceedings article. Match the task to
the genre; a short exposition does not require every step of a structural paper.

## Start With A Choice Or A Measurement Problem

For a dynamic mechanism, explain what the agent chooses, when consequences occur,
and which uncertainty matters. Only then introduce states and value functions.
MY01 and MY02 provide examples of connecting industry institutions to those
objects; MY03 p11 shows how an event-by-event interpretation can follow a value
equation. Do not import their industry assumptions into another application.

For a descriptive passage, define what an observation measures before drawing
economic conclusions. W02 distinguishes journeys, stops, and unobserved segments;
W03 separates quantities from distances. A concrete product example can carry the
reader into an unfamiliar measurement problem, but it cannot substitute for the
definition. W04 separates ownership from entry and exit status before interpreting
employment reallocation. See the [source cards](SOURCE_STUDY.md).

**Review question:** Can the reader say what is chosen or measured before seeing
the first consequential equation or estimate? If not, move a definition forward
rather than adding a generic promise to explain it later.

## Carry The Economic Object Across Levels

Keep firm responses, industry composition, and aggregate responses distinct.
Use a decomposition to show which change affects which component, then interpret
the components in the same order. L01 p18 and L03 p19 offer different examples:
aggregation weights in one case, the real-output measurement convention in the
other. A change in an average need not establish the same change within each group.

State the denominator, weighting, base year, and price convention at the point
where they become necessary. Do not introduce a new economic explanation merely
because it makes a paragraph connect more smoothly. Compare an outcome with its
empirical counterpart before calling the evidence support for the mechanism.

**Review question:** Does the next paragraph use the previous result, or quietly
change the population, denominator, price convention, or economic object?

## Separate Representation, Recovery, And Identification

A theory-consistent transformation describes an economic object conditional on
its assumptions. It is not automatically a causal design. R11 develops a matrix
representation; R12 separately addresses empirical variation. These are related
tasks, not interchangeable warrants for a claim.

MY04 makes another distinction useful in counterfactual writing: identifying a
model, a behavioral counterfactual, and welfare are different questions. Specify
which object must agree across models consistent with the observations. Explain
the content of a normalization or additional restriction before using it to
recover otherwise undetermined components.

When comparing experiments, state what is held fixed. L02 p15 explicitly separates
firm decisions at fixed prices from general-equilibrium responses. The distinction
also matters when comparing an accounting exercise with a re-solved model.

**Review question:** Is the passage interpreting data, recovering an object under
restrictions, or establishing identification? Does its verb match the task?

## Use Displays To Resolve A Specific Difficulty

Introduce an equation with the question it answers. Define objects before using
them, give the decisive relation, then explain why the relation matters. MY03 p19
labels derivative components; L04 p15 explains geometric distances; R11 p4 relates
matrix products to economic shares and substitutes a previously derived response.

An equation’s dimension, index order, normalization, and approximation remain
scientific constraints. A visually simple formula can still be wrong. Avoid moving
conditions into a footnote if the main claim would be misleading without them.
Proof ancestry and implementation detail can be footnotes when they interrupt a
complete main-text argument.

**Review question:** Can the reader explain the display in economic terms without
guessing whether the same symbol now denotes a level, derivative, or baseline share?

## Compare Evidence Before Claiming Validation

Distinguish fitted targets from untargeted outcomes and genuinely external checks.
W02 p24 is a useful presentation example: panel labels and prose identify what the
model was fitted to. Agreement with a target is not an independent identification
test. Explain which alternative mechanism or assumption a diagnostic can actually
distinguish. Preserve contrary evidence beside the claim it limits.

Literature comparisons should identify a shared problem, a changed restriction or
measurement, and the consequence for the present inference. W01 p31 attends to
units and aggregation; L02 p6 allows complementary explanations. Neither supplies
permission to claim the alternative mechanisms have been ruled out elsewhere.

**Review question:** Is the comparison about a common estimand and sample, or merely
similar-looking numbers? Does the citation support the precise claim it follows?

## Evaluate Sequence Without A Mechanical Template

At the sentence level, look for a shared economic object and a stated relationship.
At the paragraph level, check that evidence precedes the interpretation it supports.
At a subsection boundary, identify the unresolved question the next subsection
answers. Across sections, trace the inputs to the final result. See the
[six worked repairs](WORKED_EXAMPLES.md) for explicit maps and fidelity checks.

Record problems in these dimensions separately:

| Dimension | A concrete failure | What to check after editing |
|---|---|---|
| Sequence | A conclusion precedes its necessary definition | Objects, timing, and premises appear before use |
| Flow | A paragraph changes from a firm to an aggregate without explanation | The aggregation step and weights are stated |
| Argument | A plausible mechanism is reported as identified | The evidence supports the stated strength of inference |
| Mathematics | A fixed-choice derivative is called a finite welfare change | The approximation and optimizing conditions remain explicit |
| Interpretation | A decomposition is given a causal label | Accounting components and causal responses remain distinct |
| Literature | A precedent is used as empirical validation | The comparison states its shared task and changed restriction |

This review is editorial evidence, not an author-similarity score. A vocabulary
pass or a polished example cannot certify the derivation or the empirical result.
