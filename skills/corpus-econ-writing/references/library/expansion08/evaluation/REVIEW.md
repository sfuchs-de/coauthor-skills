## Extraction And Source Review

Six primary author/institution PDFs contribute 287 extracted pages. Twenty-seven
selected pages support the source cards; six selected equation pages were
rendered and visually checked. Deaton-Muellbauer's two-column text extraction
has merged words, and some Milgrom-Segal mathematical glyphs are not represented
correctly in plain text. Poppler also reported drawing warnings during extraction.
The inspected rendered equations are readable. The corpus index is not a
reliable source for reconstructing mathematics; use the PDF and explicit
derivation checks. Rare tokens, notation, names, and reference abbreviations
require context review. The 23,211 figure counts exact recognized attested forms,
not 23,211 manually approved dictionary entries.
