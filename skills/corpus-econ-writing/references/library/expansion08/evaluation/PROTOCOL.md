# Theory08 Evaluation Protocol

Frozen before revised skill instructions and worked examples. This is a local
same-context development test, not independent or blinded validation. No
unassisted-model comparison is claimed. Previous theory07 drafts stay unchanged.

Four new fictional briefs test different conditions from the earlier exercises.
Retain first drafts, lexical repairs, and explicit mathematical checks separately.
Use the new source inventory for the repaired prose; do not change inventories
to make an individual sample pass. Essential unapproved terms are reported, not
silently removed. Word counts exclude mathematical displays.

## Briefs

1. **Changing input prices (220-350 prose words).** A firm chooses a flexible
   input x with output f(L,x), fixed L, output price p, and input price r. Derive
   the optimized restricted-profit differential when both p and r change. Explain
   input adjustment, then distinguish the marginal price share from the average
   factor-income share. No functional-form assumption and no estimation claim.
2. **Policy feedback (240-380 words).** Resident welfare is w(E,T,t), with an
   explicit direct effect of the policy t. Equilibrium is F(E,T,t)=0 and the
   transfer is T=R(E,t). Derive a scalar total derivative with feedback; state
   what is held fixed in every partial derivative and the nonzero-denominator
   condition. Explain why a partial transfer value and a total value cannot be
   used interchangeably. Do not invent a calibrated or empirical result.
3. **Conditions along the argument (280-450 words).** A household chooses
   purchases from two destinations. Separate general first-order welfare
   valuation from recovering income with a common destination response. Introduce
   the latter condition only when recovery is attempted, and explain a case where
   origin-specific access changes invalidate it while the welfare envelope remains
   valid. Give a transition to estimation without claiming it identifies every
   citywide income change. No early inventory of all assumptions.
4. **Literature by economic task (180-280 words).** Use only verified source
   cards for Deaton-Muellbauer, Fajgelbaum-Khandelwal, and Fajgelbaum-Gaubert.
   Organize a literature paragraph around valuing purchases, unequal incidence,
   and endogenous spatial adjustment. State the assumptions that separate these
   tasks. Do not invent findings, compare incommensurate numerical estimates, or
   imply the sources validate a new method.

## Gates

- Raw drafts saved before checks; repairs do not replace raw files.
- Evaluate conditions, derivation, interpretation, transitions, depth, and
  literature organization separately from lexical status.
- Reject writer-facing instructions in publication prose.
- Check partial and total derivatives independently by algebra and finite
  differences in a fictional scalar example. No scientific model solves.
- Audit exact vocabulary and twelve-word prose overlap. Both are incomplete
  indicators: a lexical pass is not mathematical or editorial validation.
- Recheck the eight old repaired samples read-only and report unresolved flags.
- Test label classification, compound rejection, unknown words, routing, archive
  safety, manifest changes, and preservation of previous editions.
- Independent generation/review and visual assessment of manuscript layout
  remain outside this test unless separately authorized and performed.
