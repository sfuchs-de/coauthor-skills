# Four Connected Theory Examples

These original fictional exercises illustrate the new guide, not a revision of
the Barcelona paper or a recommendation to change its scientific procedure.
Their [briefs](evaluation/PROTOCOL.md) were frozen before instruction changes.

- [Producer envelope with changing input prices](evaluation/final/T01.tex):
  optimization, the canceled quantity term, and a marginal-versus-average share.
- [Policy and revenue feedback](evaluation/final/T02.tex): fixed arguments,
  the implicit denominator, direct welfare dependence, and a different transfer
  experiment.
- [Household valuation before response recovery](evaluation/final/T03.tex):
  general local valuation, a separately introduced common-response restriction,
  its basket adjustment, and an access-cost failure case.
- [Literature by economic task](evaluation/final/T04.tex): the connection between
  valuation, unequal expenditure incidence, and endogenous spatial adjustment.

Raw drafts remain in `evaluation/first/`. The [review](evaluation/REVIEW.md)
records what changed, including a missing condition in the first household
draft. The [release audit](evaluation/RELEASE_AUDIT.json) records exact checks;
passing those checks is not independent validation or evidence that first drafts
will reliably satisfy the skill.
