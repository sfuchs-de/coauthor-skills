# Urban Vocabulary Policy

Use this 53-paper inventory only for an explicitly selected urban writing task.
The general 45-paper default is preserved. State which inventory an audit used;
do not switch inventories merely to make a failed passage pass.

Prefer common main-text words. A normalized form attested only in a reference,
appendix, proper name, or extraction fragment is weak evidence for ordinary
prose. Inspect its page evidence before using it. Exact word attestation does not
approve a new meaning, compound, free inflection, or technical concept.

The [usage supplement](USAGE_SUPPLEMENT.md) is selective editorial guidance,
not a whitelist. Keep distinctions such as rent versus house price, flows versus
stocks, sorting versus causal effects, and assignment versus take-up even when
a less familiar term needs a narrowly justified exception. Obtain explicit
approval for an exception and record why an attested alternative would lose
meaning. This extension creates no new approved exceptions.

Check revised source modules, not just drivers. The checker does not expand
arbitrary TeX dependencies or macros. Math is not a place to hide prose. A lexical
pass certifies neither a claim, an assumption, a citation, nor an argument.

Earlier inventories, source hashes, examples, and exception records remain
frozen. Downloaded papers and full extracted text remain local; vocabulary
evidence and original editorial analyses may be packaged without their PDFs.
