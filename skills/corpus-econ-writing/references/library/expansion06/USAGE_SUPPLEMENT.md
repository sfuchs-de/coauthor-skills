# Additional Economic Usage

Usage distinctions are editorial aids, not vocabulary or concept approval.

| Term | Meaning | Distinction | Source analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| housing stock | Existing durable dwellings at a date | Not the flow of construction or households entering a city | [U01 p5](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u01) |
| replacement cost | Cost of producing a comparable housing unit | Not its current rent, transaction price, or household expenditure | [U01 p4](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u01) |
| urban decline | A specified fall in a city outcome over time | Population decline does not itself identify a productivity shock | [U01 p35](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u01) |
| housing supply elasticity | Quantity response relative to a specified price change | State units versus floorspace, horizon, and geographic scale | [U02 p4](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u02) |
| development margin | Change in parcels brought into housing production | Separate from intensity or renovation on developed parcels | [U02 p4](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u02) |
| market access | Access to workers or workplaces through specified links | Do not treat all access measures as the same equilibrium object | [U02 p16](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u02) |
| real wage | Wages relative to an explicit price measure | Not welfare including unmeasured or modeled amenities | [U03 p2](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u03) |
| amenity residual | The part of inferred location value not explained by included terms | Conditional on preferences and measurement; not direct observation of congestion or quality | [U03 p29](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u03) |
| skill sorting | Changes in where different worker groups reside | Distinct from a within-person productivity or wage change | [U03 p2](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u03) |
| rent control | A particular legal restriction and eligibility rule | Covered tenants, future renters, and landlords face different margins | [U04 p1](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u04) |
| neighborhood tenure | Time a household has lived in a neighborhood | Not building age or time since a treatment was assigned | [U04 p9](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u04) |
| rent-equivalent welfare | A utility change expressed through a specified rent adjustment | Not an observed rent change or a model-free transfer | [U04 p25](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u04) |
| central city | A defined municipal geography | Not the entire metropolitan area; preserve boundary vintage | [U05 p3](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u05) |
| highway ray | A road segment extending outward from a center | Not interchangeable with one highway crossing the city | [U05 p14](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u05) |
| historical plan | Earlier intended infrastructure used as variation | Age alone does not establish relevance or exclusion | [U05 p8](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u05) |
| traffic volume | Travel quantity in a specified vehicle-distance measure | Not travel speed, time, congestion, or welfare | [U06 p4](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u06) |
| road capacity | Measured provision of road infrastructure | Lane kilometers are not a direct measure of the cost of travel | [U06 p4](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u06) |
| induced travel | Additional travel following a change in capacity or cost | Does not on its own establish zero benefit from an expansion | [U06 p30](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u06) |
| capitalization | An attribute reflected in equilibrium housing prices | A price coefficient need not equal mean household willingness to pay | [U07 p23](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u07) |
| boundary comparison | A comparison of nearby locations across a defined boundary | Other amenities and household composition may also change there | [U07 p31](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u07) |
| residential sorting | Households selecting locations with different attributes | Not a causal neighborhood effect on a fixed population | [U07 p19](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u07) |
| intent-to-treat | Effect of assigned treatment regardless of take-up | Not the effect of actual relocation among all movers | [U08 p13](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u08) |
| childhood exposure | Time spent in an environment during childhood | Age, duration, and moving disruption require separate interpretation | [U08 p5](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u08) |
| family clustering | Inference permitting shared errors within families | The unit of random assignment matters even with child-level outcomes | [U08 p13](SOURCE_STUDY.md#u08) |
