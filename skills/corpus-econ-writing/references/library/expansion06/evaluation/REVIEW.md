# Urban Example Review

These are same-context editorial exercises, not an independent writing benchmark.
All six preserve explicit fictional premises rather than substituting findings
from the source papers. The [worked examples](../WORKED_EXAMPLES.md) include the
intended inference sequence and forbidden strengthening for each repair.

| Example | Core fidelity check | Remaining scope |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Short-horizon durability does not become immediate demolition | No universal supply elasticity or causal productivity estimate |
| E02 | Tenant expenditure and owner receipts remain different objects | No citywide welfare sign before common ownership accounting |
| E03 | Boundary price differences remain descriptive until restrictions are assessed | No causal school effect or mean preference estimate |
| E04 | Earlier plans require relevance and exclusion | No declaration that historical variation is automatically valid |
| E05 | Travel quantity does not establish travel cost or welfare | No inferred congestion or benefit sign |
| E06 | Assignment, take-up, population, age, and exposure remain separate | No all-mover effect or independently isolated duration mechanism |

The first lexical run passed five of six examples. It rejected the exact form
`plan's`; the revised fourth example uses `the age of the plan`, without changing
meaning. The [first receipt](FIRST_AUDIT.json) remains preserved. The
[checked receipt](CHECKED_AUDIT.json) records the revised lexical and local source
overlap checks. No new exception was approved.

A 12-word exact-window screen checks copying only at that window length. It is
not a semantic originality score, an authorship test, or a proof that shorter
phrases do not overlap. Portable replay verifies recorded texts and vocabulary;
the full source screen requires the local downloaded papers and is not rerun
offline. Independent collaborator or model evaluation remains pending.
