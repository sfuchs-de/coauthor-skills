# Comparing Authors and Genres

## Attribution and Selection

This purposive sample covers eight papers involving Donaldson, six involving
Allen, eight involving Arkolakis, and seven involving Redding. These counts overlap:
there are 23 distinct papers, not 29 independent observations. The study cannot
identify who wrote a coauthored paragraph, an author's private drafting process,
or the effect of journal editing. It does not infer an individual's general style
from word frequencies. Read the comparisons below as patterns in named samples.

The mixture of published articles and author manuscripts adds another limitation.
The downloaded Universal Gravity version is from 2018, for example, while its
publication year is 2020. A revision history could change the exposition. Exact
source versions and hashes therefore remain attached to every card.

## Donaldson Sample

**Useful emphasis:** the path from an economic question through observable objects
to an answer. D01 connects numbered model implications to empirical operations.
D03 makes representation, identification, and practical estimation distinct stages.
D04 asks whether a model answers a particular causal question, not whether it
matches everything. These are joint-writing observations where applicable.

**Apply it:** let the reader see what each stage contributes. Explain institutional
details when they create variation or threaten identification. Interpret a model
comparison by the question it tests. Keep relative and aggregate claims separate.

**Do not imitate mechanically:** historical detail, a long introduction roadmap,
or a validation exercise is not useful merely because it appears in a strong paper.
Retain the material serving the current argument.

Entry points: [D01](reviews/D01.md), [D03](reviews/D03.md), [D04](reviews/D04.md),
and the explanatory essay [D05](reviews/D05.md).

## Allen Sample

**Useful emphasis:** translate interactions into an organized system, then explain
what its equations permit. A01 separates economic and geographic components; A03
combines related models before introducing empirical tools. A05 returns from a
search model to the empirical patterns that motivated it. A06 carefully separates
different notions of persistence and uniqueness.

**Apply it:** identify the common objects before consolidating model variants.
Explain the economic operation represented by an operator. Give parameter conditions
a task and a consequence. Use data facts to motivate a mechanism without claiming
the facts alone prove it.

**Do not imitate mechanically:** a common mathematical representation can hide
different welfare meanings or closure assumptions. State those distinctions. A
compact theorem or diagram can still need more space to be readable.

Entry points: [A01](reviews/A01.md), [A03](reviews/A03.md), [A05](reviews/A05.md),
[A06](reviews/A06.md).

## Arkolakis Sample

**Useful emphasis:** a comparison model makes the new mechanism legible. K01 starts
with a simple case before presenting aggregate restrictions. K02 and K03 connect
firm choices to specific empirical patterns. K04 separates common macro predictions
from different welfare implications.

**Apply it:** explain what the benchmark misses; introduce the margin that resolves
the mismatch; state which aggregate results change and which do not. Map calibration
targets to parameters. Keep empirical discrepancies visible instead of describing
every comparison as successful.

**Do not imitate mechanically:** a formula valid for a named class is not a universal
sufficient statistic. Micro-level realism need not increase the measured aggregate
effect. Neither functional-form generality nor a calibrated fit eliminates the
need for an identification argument.

Entry points: [K01](reviews/K01.md), [K02](reviews/K02.md), [K03](reviews/K03.md),
[K04](reviews/K04.md).

## Redding Sample

**Useful emphasis:** precise spatial objects and recursive recovery. The Berlin
paper distinguishes recovery of characteristics from estimation of externalities.
The London paper adds structure in stages. The commuting paper carries residence
and workplace distinctions through its evidence. The price-index paper explains
what normalization and residual interpretation contribute. The itinerary paper
defines a new travel object before using it.

**Apply it:** carry spatial indices and denominators consistently. Tell the reader
what has been learned before introducing additional assumptions. Label observed,
imputed, inverted, and simulated series separately. Give maps economic interpretation
without overstating mechanism identification.

**Do not imitate mechanically:** extended model definitions and repeated condition
lists can slow a shorter argument. Keep the economically necessary detail, but
consolidate genuinely repeated explanations. Do not copy a publisher's typography
or an old paper's parameter values as a house convention.

Entry points: [R01](reviews/R01.md), [R02](reviews/R02.md), [R03](reviews/R03.md),
[R04](reviews/R04.md), [R07](reviews/R07.md).

## The More Important Comparison: Genre

| Writing task | Useful approach | Necessary protection |
|---|---|---|
| General theory | Define primitives, state conditions, interpret results | Do not conceal restrictions in an illustration |
| Empirical design | Explain records and threats, then the identifying variation | Descriptive patterns do not establish exclusion |
| Quantitative application | Separate recovery, calibration, validation, counterfactual | A matched baseline is not an independent test |
| Review essay | Use familiar choices, examples, and diagrams | Do not remove conditions essential to the explanation |
| Method paper | Distinguish the estimand, identification, implementation, and test | A computable estimate is not automatically identified |

The main difference is the reader's task, not a ranking of writing styles. A
diagram-heavy essay and a formal research article can explain the same economics
at different levels. For the BCN paper, preserve the derivation needed to justify
recovery; for its short slides, an overview and worded equations may carry the
sequence, with formal details in linked backups. That is a recommendation, not
an edit to either deliverable.

## What Not to Infer

- The most frequent word is not necessarily the clearest word.
- An observed phrase is not a mandatory phrase or permission to copy it.
- Published prose may retain long sentences, signposts, and terms often labeled AI-like.
- Eliminating those terms indiscriminately can remove technical content.
- A short main text can depend on substantial appendices; that is not proof that detail is dispensable.
- Successful publication does not make every table label, footnote, or causal claim exemplary.
- These page-based reviews do not independently validate the papers' numerical results.

Use the sample to learn operations: motivate, define, derive, identify, interpret,
qualify, and connect. Preserve an author's own fluid language where it performs
those operations well.
