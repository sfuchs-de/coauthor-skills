# Additional Economic Usage

Thirty-six distinctions supplement the original 185 entries. Editorial labels are not a whitelist, and examples are not new scientific results. Exact phrase attestation must be checked separately from the presence of component words.

| Term | Meaning | Distinction to preserve | Source analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| transition path | A sequence of equilibrium states following specified initial conditions and shocks. | Not the terminal steady state or the number of solver iterations. | [R08 p20](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r08) |
| steady state | A state reproduced over time under a specified unchanged environment. | A unique transition conditional on initial conditions need not imply a unique steady state. | [D08 p3](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d08) |
| initial conditions | States fixed at the beginning of the dynamic exercise. | They differ from a sequence of exogenous fundamentals. | [R08 p3](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r08) |
| contemporaneous spillovers | Effects of activity on others within the same period. | Do not combine with lagged effects when conditions use them differently. | [D08 p3](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d08) |
| historical spillovers | Effects entering through activity in earlier periods. | Persistent outcomes do not by themselves identify this channel. | [D08 p3](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d08) |
| forward-looking decisions | Choices depending on expected future payoffs. | Not simply a static decision repeated over time. | [R08 p4](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r08) |
| labor force participation | A choice about whether to work in the modeled labor market. | Not a move between workplaces or an intensive hours response. | [K05 p5](SOURCE_STUDY.md#k05) |
| reservation wage | The payoff threshold governing work participation in the stated model. | Its response and normalization matter for aggregate labor responses. | [K05 p17](SOURCE_STUDY.md#k05) |
| efficiency units | Labor services after a specified individual productivity adjustment. | Worker counts, observed earnings, and the price of an efficiency unit differ. | [K05 p17](SOURCE_STUDY.md#k05) |
| observed average earnings | The measured mean of payments received by the observed workers. | Selection can make its change differ from a wage-rate change. | [K05 p17](SOURCE_STUDY.md#k05) |
| producer price | The price relevant to the producer's receipts in the stated tax system. | It can differ from gross consumer payments. | [D07 p9](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d07) |
| consumer price | The price entering the consumer's budget and purchases. | State whether the price includes taxes and transport costs. | [D07 p9](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d07) |
| tax wedge | A specified gap between prices or returns faced by different agents. | A large wedge alone does not establish a large feasible welfare gain. | [D07 p5](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d07) |
| policy instrument | A variable the government is allowed to choose. | A restricted instrument set changes the policy problem. | [D07 p10](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d07) |
| unilateral policy | One government's choice taking other governments' policies as given. | Not a coordinated world optimum or a retaliation equilibrium. | [D07 p10](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d07) |
| scale elasticity | The proportional productivity response to sector size under the specified technology. | A calibrated trade elasticity can enter both its recovery and policy consequences. | [D07 p35](SOURCE_STUDY.md#d07) |
| excess labor demand | A demand-minus-supply relationship in the specified labor-market system. | A shock exposure defined from it is not an observed equilibrium employment change. | [K05 p3](SOURCE_STUDY.md#k05) |
| differential effect | A response contrast across units under a declared comparison. | Does not alone supply a common aggregate response. | [K05 p2](SOURCE_STUDY.md#k05) |
| aggregate effect | A response aggregated with declared weights and support. | A missing common component needs its own recovery restriction. | [K05 p2](SOURCE_STUDY.md#k05) |
| impact matrix | A model-specific mapping from shocks into an initial response. | Not the operator updating that response through time. | [R08 p20](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r08) |
| transition matrix | A model-specific operator governing the next dynamic state. | Not every spending-share matrix has this meaning. | [R08 p20](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r08) |
| eigenshock | A shock whose initial impact lies in a specified eigenvector direction. | Not an independently identified empirical shock without further evidence. | [R08 p20](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r08) |
| eigencomponent | A response component in a declared spectral decomposition. | The decomposition is not automatically a behavioral microfoundation. | [R08 p20](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r08) |
| elasticity bound | A uniform restriction on the magnitude of a derivative over a stated domain. | A local estimate at one point need not establish a global bound. | [A07 p6](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a07) |
| sufficient condition | A condition that guarantees a specified conclusion. | Its failure does not establish failure of the conclusion. | [A07 p7](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a07) |
| globally necessary | A property required to guarantee a conclusion across the stated class. | Distinguish existence of a counterexample from failure in every economy. | [A07 p7](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a07) |
| up to scale | Equivalent solutions remain after a scalar change in the stated object. | A normalization selects units, not an independently measured level. | [A07 p9](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a07) |
| best response | A choice maximizing the agent's objective given specified other choices. | A numerical iteration has this interpretation only when its mapping matches those choices. | [A07 p9](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a07) |
| adding-up restriction | A sum constraint implied by the declared shares or account. | An aggregate cancellation needs the correct weights and closure. | [A08 p44](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a08) |
| import share | Purchases from an origin divided by the destination's declared expenditure. | It is not the origin's share of sales to that destination. | [A08 p43](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a08) |
| export share | Sales to a destination divided by the origin's declared total sales. | State subscript direction and the denominator when moving to matrices. | [A08 p43](SOURCE_STUDY.md#a08) |
| structural residual | An unobserved model object recovered to rationalize observed outcomes. | Its name is not independent evidence of a primitive or mechanism. | [R10 p22](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r10) |
| spatial scale | The geographic resolution at which choices, links, and outcomes are modeled. | A regional parameter need not apply unchanged within a city. | [R10 p3](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r10) |
| consumption access | Access relevant to purchases or consumption travel under the stated model. | Not workplace access or generic market access without qualification. | [R10 p42](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r10) |
| spatial sorting | Allocation of heterogeneous agents across locations through specified choices. | A difference in tract averages does not identify individual responses. | [R10 p61](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r10) |
| within-sample shock | An observed change in the model's empirical domain used to evaluate predicted responses. | Disclosure of estimation targets remains necessary before calling a comparison independent. | [R10 p6](SOURCE_STUDY.md#r10) |
