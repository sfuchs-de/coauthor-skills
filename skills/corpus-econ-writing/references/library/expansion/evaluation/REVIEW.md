# Longer Writing Evaluation

## What Was Tested

Four original fictional exercises cover a theory subsection, a data-to-estimation
handoff, a literature comparison, and a short results section. They contain 400,
434, 312, and 505 approximate prose words after revision. They are longer than the
previous paragraph exercises, but are not a test of a complete manuscript.

The brief, first drafts, initial instruction hashes, an explicit instruction
amendment, and intermediate audits are preserved. Ten short worked revisions
add examples at each level of the argument. Their publication-like revised text
is checked separately from the editorial descriptions of deliberately weak text.

## Argument Review

- **Structure:** Each exercise has an economic task and a dependent next task.
  The results exercise keeps adverse sensitivity beside the mean before models.
- **Flow:** Paragraph openings inherit purchases, responses, or a comparison.
  They do not merely repeat summaries with new headings.
- **Argument:** General welfare valuation is separated from response recovery,
  identification, and structural policy assumptions.
- **Exposition:** Choices and timing precede the envelopes. Assumptions are
  introduced where they resolve an ambiguity rather than in an unmotivated list.
- **Interpretation:** Sign, rank, units, and precision have distinct meanings.
  A descriptive mismatch motivates links without identifying displacement.
- **Math and sequence:** The budget movement equals the local welfare expression.
  Fixed labor quantity is not silently made a fixed wage. Matrix inversion and
  convergence remain separate. Displays receive interpretation before use.
- **Literature:** Comparisons identify the shared task, changed restriction, and
  resulting inference. Citations do not validate the identifying condition.

The substantive review found two defects in the first drafts: an implication of
fixed wages, and an invented transmission claim in the conclusion. Both were
removed. These are fidelity failures, not small vocabulary penalties.

## Ratings And Limits

The [ratings](RATINGS.json) use seven dimensions on a 0-4 editorial scale, with
inapplicable literature scores left blank. The final overall self-rating is about
9.0/10. This is an assessment of these manually revised exercises, not independent
validation, an author-identification score, model fine-tuning, or demonstrated
improvement over the earlier rounds. The briefs, generator, and reviewer share
context. No independent agents or reviewers were used.

Remaining weaknesses are concrete: the methods passage is dense; the literature
passage retains several author groups; and the final theory handoff lacks the
actual following regression. No style score removes those limitations. In a real
paper, the next operation should be made explicit using that paper's actual model.

## Vocabulary And Scientific Checks

The four first drafts failed the lexical gate. All checked passages pass the
31-paper inventory without new exceptions and fall inside the requested ranges.
No screened 12-word exact source match was found. These checks do not establish
authorship, mathematical validity, or copyright compliance on their own.

The stricter inventory still requires a repair pass, even with 2,709 additional
attested forms. Whole-sentence revisions preserve distinctions rather than hiding
technical prose in math. Low-frequency context flags remain review items.

Independent numerical calculations check two envelopes in one smooth illustrative
economy, local second-order errors, an inverse with divergent feedback, income
minus cost arithmetic, tax percentage-point units, and rank agreement despite
opposite signs. They are tests of these fixtures, not proofs for the BCN model
or independent checks of source-paper results.

## Delivery Scope

The expanded resource adds eight local PDFs, 502 extracted pages, selected
passages on 68 pages, and one visually inspected page per added paper. All original
corpus artifacts in the preservation receipt remain unchanged. Source papers,
full text, and rendered pages are excluded from the portable release.

No manuscript, slide, regression, equilibrium solve, publication profile, GitHub,
Dropbox, or Overleaf file is changed. Independent evaluation and collaborator
setup remain pending until actually performed.
