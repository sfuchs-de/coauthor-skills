# Argument Maps For The Longer Exercises

These maps describe the revised fictional exercises, not the BCN manuscript.
They make dependency and flow checks visible without putting the editing ledger
into research prose. Each map has a claim, support, and a remaining task.

## L01: Two Envelopes To An Empirical Task

**Dependency:** household and firm choices -> timing and fixed prices -> input
optimality -> producer envelope -> income allocation -> household envelope ->
budget movement -> finite-change qualification -> response recovery.

| Passage | Job | Depends on | Passes forward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Separate receipt and purchase exposure | The price shock | Need to value both effects |
| Firm problem | Define choices and G | Fixed L0 and r, flexible x | An optimized return |
| Producer derivative | Explain the vanishing input term | Interior optimum | Initial-output income exposure |
| Household result | Subtract initial purchase cost | General optimized preferences, fixed income share | Local welfare expression |
| Geometry and handoff | Explain movement at initial consumption | The two envelopes | Missing causal income and price responses |

**Sentence chain near the main result:** income is allocated -> budget is defined
-> optimization supplies local valuation -> initial output and consumption have
distinct jobs -> location alone cannot determine incidence.

**Scope check:** fixed workplace quantity does not require a fixed wage; initial
choice terms vanish locally, but choices can change; finite gains need more than
the first-order expression. The last sentence leaves an actual empirical task.

## L02: Descriptive Contrast To Identification

**Dependency:** contrasting spatial patterns -> different record links -> plausible
transmission and alternatives -> constructed exposures -> identifying restriction
-> demand correction -> limits of observed-income validation.

The first paragraph establishes the contrast but does not identify displacement.
The second explains why origin, work, and purchase links matter and why common
seasonality remains a concern. The third carries that concern into the regression.
The fourth distinguishes a spending response from the income object required by
welfare. Thus the passage does not end at “we run a regression.”

**Remaining weakness:** the method paragraph is dense. A real paper needs its actual
sample, notation, and supported correction, not a general reference to a restriction.

## L03: Literature As A Sequence Of Different Tasks

**Dependency:** local valuation versus aggregate sufficient information -> demand
recovery and normalization -> spatial representation and equilibrium properties
-> empirical identifying conditions.

Paragraph one says what is valued and what aggregate restrictions permit.
Paragraph two asks what observed spending can recover. Paragraph three explains
the extra spatial task and why a theorem cannot supply exogeneity. This is a
comparison by inferential operation, not a ranking of named authors.

**Citation check:** each attributed source distinction comes from the brief and
reviewed source analogue. The exercise makes no priority claim and does not
attribute unrestricted household valuation to the aggregate trade result.

## L04: Baseline, Adverse Sensitivity, Then Additional Structure

**Dependency:** common population and shock -> income minus cost -> changed
recovery mean -> preserved ordering versus sign reversal -> limits of validation
-> finite model comparison -> feasible tax comparison -> conditional answer.

The baseline paragraph creates the income-recovery question. The sensitivity
subsection answers it before the structural exercise introduces further assumptions.
The model paragraph distinguishes endogenous workplace choice from hours and
finite percentages from local log points. The tax paragraph explains the policy
premium without inventing a district solution or a sparsity mechanism.

**Conclusion check:** every concluding claim is present in the supplied brief or
follows from its arithmetic. The first draft's new transmission finding was removed.
The model comparison does not erase the adverse empirical sensitivity.
